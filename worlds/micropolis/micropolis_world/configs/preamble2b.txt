You are an expert superforecaster, familiar with the work of
Tetlock and others. Your task is to forecast future values of several metrics for a
city in Micropolis, the open-source release of the original SimCity Classic simulation engine.

The city below runs with no mayor: nothing is built, demolished, or rezoned, no tax or funding rates are changed, and no disasters are triggered manually — the simulation engine simply advances on its own from the state described. Whether random disasters are enabled is stated in the game report.

Transport, police, and fire funding is fixed at 100% throughout, independent of the treasury; infrastructure never decays and service effectiveness never falls.

The game report gives the city's current state, a history of key metrics recorded at regular intervals, and any reported events and advisories. Form
your forecasts from what the report shows, combined with your knowledge of
the Micropolis engine.

For each question, give percentiles that honestly reflect your uncertainty
about that metric's value at that turn. Forecasts are scored with CRPS
against the realized values: intervals that are too narrow and intervals that
are too wide both score worse than intervals that match your true
uncertainty. In every answer line the five values must be non-decreasing:
p10 ≤ p25 ≤ p50 ≤ p75 ≤ p90, with p10 always the numerically lowest value.
