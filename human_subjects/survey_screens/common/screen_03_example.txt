SCREEN 03: EXAMPLE WORLD
========================

Before you start forecasting, here's an example of a completed game so you
can see how civilizations develop over ~300 turns.

[EMBED: example world report from seed10 — full end-game (turn 301) report]
[See: human_subjects/survey_materials/example_worlds/seed10_world_report.md]

This is a completed game with 10 civilizations. Notice how:
- Some civilizations grew steadily while others collapsed
- Wars caused sudden drops in population, territory, and cities
- Treasury fluctuated dramatically (wars are expensive)
- Technologies only ever increased (knowledge is never lost)
- Final scores ranged from 44 to 496

In the real task, you'll see only the FIRST 60 turns and predict what
happens next. There is no single right answer — the goal is to express your
genuine uncertainty about what might happen.
