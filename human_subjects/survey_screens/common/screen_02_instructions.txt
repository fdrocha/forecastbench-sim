SCREEN 02: INSTRUCTIONS
=======================

## About This Game

FreeCiv is a turn-based strategy game inspired by the Civilization series.
Each game simulates multiple civilizations developing on a shared map over
hundreds of turns.

### How the game works

- Civilizations start with a single settler and must found cities, research
  technologies, and manage resources.
- Turns advance simultaneously for all civilizations. The game runs
  autonomously (AI-controlled players).
- Civilizations interact through diplomacy, trade, and warfare. Wars can
  lead to city capture, territory loss, and population collapse.

### Reading the world report

You will see a "world report" — a snapshot of the game at turn 60 (out of
~300 total turns). Here's what each metric means:

- **Treasury (gold):** Gold reserves. Accumulates from trade; can deplete
  rapidly during wars or government transitions.
- **Cities:** Number of cities. Increases from founding; decreases if
  captured by rivals.
- **Techs:** Technologies discovered. Follows a research tree; never
  decreases (knowledge is not lost).
- **Population:** Total population across all cities. Grows with city
  development; drops sharply if cities are captured or destroyed.
- **Territory:** Tiles controlled. Expands with new cities and border
  growth; shrinks if cities are lost.
- **Score:** Composite metric reflecting overall civilization development.
- **Government:** Political system (Despotism, Republic, etc.). Affects
  economic output and military capability. Transitions cause temporary
  "Anarchy" (zero output).

Note: Civilizations with 0 population and "Anarchy" government at turn 60
have not yet been founded — they may emerge later in the game.

### What you will be doing

You will read a world report showing the first 60 turns, then forecast what
happens to one civilization at future turns (turn 90, 150, 180, and 240).

For each question, you'll distribute probability across outcome ranges using
a histogram. We'll show you how this works on the next screen.
