SCREEN 04: HOW TO MAKE PREDICTIONS
===================================

For each question, you'll see a histogram with 5 outcome ranges (bins).
Your job is to assign a probability (%) to each bin, expressing how likely
you think the true outcome will fall in that range.

### Rules

- Your percentages MUST sum to 100%
- Higher % = you think that range is more likely
- You can put 0% on bins you think are very unlikely
- You can put most of the weight on one bin if you're confident

### Example

"How much gold will the Afghani civilization have at turn 150?"

Bins: [0–500] [500–1000] [1000–1500] [1500–2000] [2000+]

If you think they'll probably have between 500 and 1000 gold but there's
some chance of more:

  [0–500]:      10%
  [500–1000]:   45%
  [1000–1500]:  30%
  [1500–2000]:  10%
  [2000+]:       5%
  Total:       100%  ✓

If you're very uncertain and think any outcome is roughly equally likely:

  [0–500]:      20%
  [500–1000]:   20%
  [1000–1500]:  20%
  [1500–2000]:  20%
  [2000+]:      20%
  Total:       100%  ✓

### Tips

- There are no trick questions. Use the world report to inform your judgment.
- It's okay to be uncertain — that's what we're measuring.
- Some metrics are more predictable than others. Technologies tend to grow
  steadily; treasury can swing wildly.
